About Mael

Mael is an undergraduate at Cornell University studying Applied Economics and Management in the Dyson School. He’s from the Bronx and proudly Dominican.

He learned how to cut hair from his uncle and has been sharpening his skills ever since. As a barber, Mael focuses on clean, personalized cuts and creating a comfortable vibe for every client.
